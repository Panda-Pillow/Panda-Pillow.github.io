My Screening Schedule — BIFF Ticket Viewer

Applies to versions: 0.1.4–0.1.6
Last updated: October 7, 2026
Effective: October 7, 2026
Developer: Panda Pillow Apps
Contact: heesu.jung.developer@gmail.com

1. Scope and purpose

This extension organizes your BIFF ticket history by screening date and restores viewing preferences and position in the same tab. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by BIFF or its ticketing provider.

2. Information processed

The extension uses the following information from the ticketing service’s list API:
• Film titles, screening dates and times, venues and auditoriums.
• Booking identifiers, ticket quantities, and booked, cancelled, or awaiting-payment status.
• Mobile-ticket links and ticket issuance quantities, cancellation quantities, and delivery methods needed to decide whether to show those links.
• Selected month, booked/cancelled view, date filter, film search text, venue, sort order, and original-view setting.
• The booking identifier used to anchor your position, the order of displayed booking identifiers, scroll position, and the item’s position on screen.

The original response may contain additional fields supplied by the service. It is read in browser memory; the extension’s list model retains only fields used for the features above. The extension does not separately ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment-card information, or call separate APIs to retrieve them. This does not guarantee that the original API never returns such fields.

3. Purposes of use

Information is used to display tickets by date, search titles, filter venues, sort results, check current booking status, link to official details and mobile tickets, and restore the list state and position in the same tab. It is not used to collect browsing history on other sites, target advertising, or profile users. The extension contains no usage analytics, error-log collection, or advertising SDKs.

4. Storage and retention

The full ticket list and mobile-ticket links remain in page memory and are not saved to an extension-operated server or permanent database. The browser’s back-forward cache may temporarily retain page memory.
Viewing preferences, anchor booking identifiers, and identifier order are stored in sessionStorage under the ticketing site’s origin. This is tab-scoped storage shared with the website, not a private extension vault. No additional application-level encryption is applied. It is generally cleared when the tab session ends, but reopening a closed tab or restoring a browser session may restore it. Closing a tab therefore does not guarantee immediate, permanent deletion.
The extension does not use Chrome Sync, chrome.storage.sync, or localStorage for persistent synchronization.

5. Network requests and disclosure

The extension sends the selected month, query criterion, history type, pagination range, and language to the ticket-list API at filmapi.maketicket.co.kr over HTTPS. Requests use credentials: include, allowing the browser to send the existing ticketing-service session to that server. The extension does not directly read passwords or cookie values and send them to the developer.
Opening details navigates to an original biff.maketicket.co.kr URL containing the booking identifier. Opening a mobile ticket opens its tket.me URL in a new tab. Mobile-ticket links may grant ticket access, so take care when sharing them.
The developer operates no collection server and does not transmit or sell ticket data, viewing preferences, or position data to the developer or advertising or analytics providers. The original site and ticket service may process IP addresses, request information, and login sessions under their own policies during direct communication. The extension does not block or manage analytics or cookies independently operated by the original site.

6. Your controls and deletion

Disable or uninstall the extension and refresh open ticket pages to stop its additional behavior. “Show original view” switches the display; it does not delete stored data or stop every request. “Reset filters” resets viewing filters, not all stored information.
To remove saved state, disable the extension and clear the ticketing site’s stored data in browser settings. Clearing site data may affect your sign-in on the original site. Uninstalling the extension alone does not guarantee immediate deletion of the website’s sessionStorage.
Booking cancellations, refunds, and requests to access or delete information held by the ticketing service are handled by that service. The extension does not automatically cancel tickets or delete the original service’s account data.

7. Limited Use

In accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy’s Limited Use requirements, use of information and its transfer to the original service are limited to providing the user-facing features described above. Information is not used for advertising, data sales, creditworthiness assessments, or lending decisions. The extension provides no feature for the developer to remotely view your ticket data.

8. Contact and changes

For feature or privacy questions, contact heesu.jung.developer@gmail.com.
Do not send actual booking identifiers, mobile-ticket URLs, passwords, or payment information. This policy describes the extension’s automatic data processing. Information you separately send through support channels is also subject to those channels’ operation and privacy information.
If data processing changes, this document will be updated and any necessary notice provided.
