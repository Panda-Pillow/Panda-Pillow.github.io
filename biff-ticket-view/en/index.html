Your day at a glance

Loads every page for the selected screening month and organizes tickets by date and time.

Find a ticket quickly

Filter by date or venue, search film titles, and sort by screening time.

Keep your place

Remembers filters and your position in the same tab, restoring them with a fresh ticket list when you return from details.

Use it on the existing ticketing site

Install in desktop Chrome, sign in to the BIFF ticketing site, and open the ticket history page. Ticket details and cancellations are handled on the official site. This extension does not automatically book or cancel tickets.

This page does not announce a public Chrome Web Store launch yet. Installation instructions and available downloads are in the public repository.

How is your information handled?

Your ticket data is not sent to a developer-operated server. Requests use your existing ticketing-service session. Viewing preferences and booking identifiers used to restore your position are stored in the site's tab-scoped session storage. No advertising or analytics SDKs are included.

Read about processing, retention, and deletion

An independently developed tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Busan International Film Festival (BIFF) or its ticketing provider. Changes to the ticketing site may affect functionality.
